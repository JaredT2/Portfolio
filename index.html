I design for the gap between lessons.

For three years I taught seven complete beginners to play guitar and piano. Almost none of them quit during a lesson. They quit in the six days afterwards, when nobody was there to tell them what to do next.

That gap is a design problem, and it is the one I keep coming back to.

About

My degree is an odd hybrid. Electrical engineering underneath, communications and media studies on top. It means I spent one semester on signal processing and the next on how people read an image, which turned out to be better preparation for design than either subject alone.

I completed the Google UX Design certificate to put method behind instincts I had already picked up teaching.

The teaching is the part that matters most to how I work. You cannot bluff a beginner. If your explanation is bad, their hand goes to the wrong fret and you find out in four seconds. Three years of that is a decent substitute for a usability lab.

I draw as well, mostly in Procreate. It is where I learned that a series holds together through restraint, not variety.
